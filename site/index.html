BIM OpenBench

Reproducible benchmarks for IFC viewers, parsers, and AI agents, on public building models with known answers.

  • Side by side: one model opened in six viewers at once, each pane the same size, with the time each took. Press Load; pick a larger model from the list.
  • View suite results: first frame and done times per viewer, model, and form, with the machine, browser, and GPU that produced them.
  • The repository: the corpus, the panes, the runner, and how to rerun everything on your own machine.
